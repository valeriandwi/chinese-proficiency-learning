Vocabulary data — licence and provenance
=======================================

The vocabulary in content/hsk2.0/level-2.yaml … level-6.yaml is derived from the
published HSK 2.0 (汉语水平考试) level word lists, as collected by the Complete HSK
Vocabulary project.

    Source:  https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary
    Files:   wordlists/exclusive/old/{2,3,4,5,6}.json  ("exclusive" = the words
             newly introduced at that level, which is what a level's syllabus is)
    Fetched: 2026-10-13
    Licence: MIT

The word lists themselves are published by Chinese Testing International / the
Center for Language Education and Cooperation; HSK is a trademark of the
administering body. This application is not endorsed by them — see
docs/sources.md §2.

How to regenerate
-----------------

    mkdir -p .local/wordlists
    for l in 2 3 4 5 6; do
      curl -sSfL -o .local/wordlists/old-$l.json \
        https://raw.githubusercontent.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary/main/wordlists/exclusive/old/$l.json
    done
    go run ./cmd/contentgen -levels 2,3,4,5
    go run ./cmd/contentctl validate content/hsk2.0/level-2.yaml

The raw JSON stays out of the repository (it is fetched into the gitignored
.local/); what is committed is the derived spine above, which is what the MIT
notice covers.
